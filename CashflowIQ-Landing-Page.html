Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is CashflowIQ?

CashflowIQ is a simplified financial management platform designed to help small businesses organise transactions, monitor recorded cash position and understand financial activity.

Which payment methods can I record?

The platform is designed to organise transactions across M-Pesa, cash and bank. Automatic synchronisation should not be assumed; availability depends on implemented integrations.

Is CashflowIQ a full accounting or tax platform?

No. CashflowIQ focuses on transaction management, financial visibility, accountability and practical financial insights. It is not positioned as a replacement for full accounting, tax or payroll systems.

Can employees submit transactions?

The planned employee workflow allows employees to scan a business QR code, enter transaction details and submit supporting receipts for the business to review.

How do I get started?

Request a demonstration. The team can walk you through the platform and explain the free-trial arrangements.

Does CashflowIQ connect automatically to my bank or M-Pesa?

Do not assume automatic connections. The available capture and integration methods will be confirmed during the product demonstration.
